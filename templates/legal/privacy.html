Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

1. What We Collect

Your account details (name, phone, email, teaching profile, optional payment details like UPI ID), and the records you create: students, parent contact details, schedules, attendance, and fee entries. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and verified email from Google — never your Google password.

2. How We Use It

Only to run TuitionPe for you: showing your dashboard, generating receipts and reports, and sending the reminders you trigger or schedule. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Messages & Reminders

WhatsApp reminders open in your own WhatsApp with a pre-filled message — we never message parents directly. Email receipts and schedule reminders are sent from the service only when you enable or trigger them.

4. Storage & Security

Data is stored in our application database. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text. Profile images may be stored with our media hosting provider (Cloudinary).

5. Your Control

You can edit or delete students and their records at any time; permanent deletion removes their payments, schedules, and attendance. To delete your entire account and data, contact us via Help & Support in Settings.

6. Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in and a local preference for your light/dark theme. No third-party tracking cookies.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will note it on this page with a new "last updated" date.
